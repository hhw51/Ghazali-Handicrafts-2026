## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges avant-garde sculptural geometry with the soulful legacy of high-end craft traditions—specifically evoking the earthen resonance of Multani tilework, Swati walnut grain, and hand-beaten brassware. It addresses discerning collectors, design curators, and cultural preservationists who look for tactile authenticity through an uncompromisingly modern lens.

The visual style is an **Experimental Archival Minimalist** aesthetic. Rather than mimicking a conventional museum catalog or a rustic marketplace, the interface treats heritage as living modern sculpture:
- **Tension of Scales:** Radical contrasts between the wide, architectural display typography of Syne and the classical, poetic cadence of Libre Caslon Text.
- **Materiality & Restraint:** Generous negative space framed with delicate hairline delineations, prioritizing warm earth, kiln-fired hues, and rich archival pigments over synthetic gradients.
- **Editorial Subversion:** Asymmetrical editorial layouts, deliberately paced content tiers, and unhurried reading rhythms that elevate artisanal artifacts to high art.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from earthen kilns, natural dye vats, and mineral veins:

- **Primary (`#C47847` - Terracotta):** Represents raw terracotta pottery and baked earth. Used as the anchor for primary interactive callouts, active indicators, and high-impact graphic marks.
- **Secondary (`#1B365D` - Lapis Heritage Indigo):** Drawn from Multani blue tile glazes and lapis lazuli inlays. Applied to critical emphasis markers, refined tags, and contrast backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#1E4B3E` - Forest Emerald / Spruce):** Reflects Swati river valleys and rich patinated wood stains. Applied for secondary interactive states, botanical provenance notes, and quiet contextual flags.
- **Neutral (`#2D2520` - Charred Walnut):** The primary text and line anchor. Replaces synthetic black with a warm, charcoal-brown hue to maintain archival warmth.
- **Canvas & Surface Tier (`#FAF8F5`, `#F5F0EA`, `#EAE2D8`):** Off-white base surfaces evocative of unbleached raw silk, lime-plastered walls, and honed Makrana marble.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5DDD0`):** Extremely quiet structural lines that frame elements like archival mounting boards.
- **Brass Accent (`#B38743`):** Reserved for authenticated maker badges, seals, and bespoke editorial accents.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances raw, wide-aperture display forms with scholarly serif prose:

- **Headlines (`Syne`):** Used with deliberate scale. The extended, avant-garde forms of Syne lend an architectural, sculpted presence to titles, series names, and exhibition markers. Display sizes command attention like stone-carved inscriptions in a contemporary gallery.
- **Body & Prose (`Libre Caslon Text`):** Evokes centuries of archival scholarship, historical tracts, and artisanal provenance records. Generous line-heights ensure effortless, immersive reading of artisan narratives, material origin essays, and technique documentations.
- **Labels & Metadata (`Syne` uppercase tracking):** All operational microcopy, category badges, technical specs, and form identifiers utilize medium-to-bold weights of Syne with wide letter tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`). This creates crisp functional clarity without disrupting the organic tone of the Caslon body text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on an editorial, asymmetrical grid inspired by high-end monograph folios:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with wide `2rem` gutters and generous `4rem` lateral margins. Promotes off-axis image-to-text placements, generous blank modules, and sculptural asymmetry.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` canvas margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen edges, ensuring uninterrupted vertical scroll flow.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections rely on multiples of `space-xl` (`3rem`, `6rem`, `9rem`) to instill a museum-like contemplative pace, preventing visual clutter around intricate physical crafts.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Components use internal padding derived from `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.75rem`), giving every piece of metadata sufficient room to exist uncrowded.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on physical framing, tonal layering, and delicate tactile contours:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Elevation is established by shifting between organic surface tiers. Base canvas (`#FAF8F5`) houses nested content panels in `#F5F0EA` (Layer 1) and archival mounts in `#EAE2D8` (Layer 2).
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth and boundary are articulated with crisp, 1px borders in `#E5DDD0`. This mirrors archival matting boards and delicate wood joinery.
- **Warm Ambient Shadow (Floating States Only):** When an element must lift off the surface during drag, hover, or modal dialogs, use an ultra-diffused, pigment-tinted shadow:
  - `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(45, 37, 32, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 37, 32, 0.04)`
  The shadow is directly tinted with `#2D2520` (Charred Walnut), grounding it naturally into the parchment background without cold gray artifacts.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a curated balance of soft structural geometry (Roundedness Level 2):

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used across input fields, actionable chips, select controls, and primary buttons. It softens hard digital corners, evoking the hand-smoothed edges of hand-carved wood or shaped clay.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Applied to exhibition cards, material sample blocks, and media frames.
- **Hero/Modal Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`):** Reserved for key modal sheets, curated collection drawers, and featured artisan spotlights.
- **Ornamental Accents:** Architectural arches and asymmetric curved viewports can be selectively used for editorial imagery masks, honoring historic Mughal jali cutouts and Multani vaulted niches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta background (`#C47847`), bone text (`#FAF8F5`), `0.5rem` border radius. Font: `Syne`, 700 weight, uppercase tracking (`0.08em`). Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover shifts to `#1B365D` (Heritage Indigo) with a smooth 200ms ease, reflecting metamorphic craft changes.
- **Secondary (Hairline):** Transparent background, 1px solid border (`#C47847` or `#2D2520`), text matching the border color. On hover, fills with `#F5F0EA`.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Inset 0-padding, `Syne` 700 uppercase with an active hairline underline positioned 4px below baseline in `#B38743` (Brass).

### Chips & Badges
- **Material & Origin Chips:** Background in `#F5F0EA`, border 1px solid `#E5DDD0`, typography in `Syne` label-sm (`#2D2520`).
- **Heritage Status Badges:** Solid `#1B365D` or `#1E4B3E` with text in `#FAF8F5`, conveying provenance (e.g., "MULTAN BLUE POTTERY", "SWAT VALLEY WALNUT"). Height: 24px, uppercase, tracking `0.1em`.

### Cards (Artifact & Editorial)
- **Artisan Card:** Outer container built with `#FAF8F5`, framed with a 1px `#E5DDD0` border and `1rem` radius. Visuals sit on an uncropped `#F5F0EA` bed with `1rem` interior inset padding. Titles in `Syne` headline-sm, accompanied by medium body text in `Libre Caslon Text` denoting artisan lineage.
- **Hover Behavior:** Translates upward by 2px with an accompanying warm ambient shadow tinted in walnut.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Enclosed in `#FAF8F5` with a 1px `#E5DDD0` resting border, `0.5rem` border-radius. Field labels use `Syne` label-md uppercase positioned above the field in `#2D2520`. Focus states transition the border to `#C47847` with a zero-spread 1px ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-finished visual rhythm. Resting state uses a 1px border in `#C47847`. Checked state fills with `#C47847` featuring a bone-white `#FAF8F5` check icon or brass center dot.

### Lists & Ledger Spec Sheets
- **Archival Specification List:** Clean tabular presentation with hairline horizontal dividers (`1px solid #E5DDD0`). Left column displays label metadata in `Syne` label-md (`#2D2520`), right column contains provenance and dimensional detail in `Libre Caslon Text` body-md.

### Bespoke System Component: Provenance Seal
- **Maker Authenticity Seal:** A dual-ringed stamp component incorporating brass accents (`#B38743`), Syne radial or condensed uppercase notation, and artisan guild indexing numbers. Affixed to high-value craft piece views.
